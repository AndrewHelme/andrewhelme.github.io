Someone has to watch the books.

County property taxes went up 17% this year — right after reassessment doubled some bills. Jamie Collins warned this would happen in 2022. He’s running with the plan to make sure it doesn’t happen again.

Who was watching? Nobody. That’s the job I’m running for.

It’s not about party. It’s about New Castle County.

We don’t have Republican taxpayers and Democratic taxpayers. We have taxpayers. We don’t have Republican parks and Democratic paramedics — we have ours, and they answer to all of us.

However you vote for governor or for Congress, a council that votes twelve to one deserves one independent set of eyes on the money. You don’t have to change parties to want that. You just have to open your tax bill.

What I’ll do

Four commitments

1. Audit first. Taxes last.

The first comprehensive, independent, public performance audit of county government in years. Every department, every contract, every dollar — results published where residents can read them. Eliminate waste before asking taxpayers for more. See the full plan →

2. Neighborhoods first.

Growth that respects the communities already here, and a councilman who always casts a clear yes or no. Permitting that is fast and predictable for homeowners and small businesses — and no fast lane for major rezonings.

3. Services you can count on.

Paramedic response times, county police, parks, libraries, sewers and stormwater. You’re paying 17% more. Your services should show it.

4. Every acre. Every dollar.

Turn mowing bills into wildflower meadows. Converting selected county roadsides and stormwater basins to native plantings means far fewer mowings, lower maintenance costs, better stormwater absorption, and habitat for monarchs and honeybees. Public land should work for the public.

Three questions before spending your money

  • Will it work?
  • Do we need it?
  • Can we afford it?

If the answer to any one of them is no, we think twice before spending a dollar that belongs to somebody else.
