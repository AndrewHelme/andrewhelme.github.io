Delaware general election · New Castle County

Tuesday, November 3

Polls are open 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Bring a neighbor.

Dates to keep 2026

Oct 10

Voter registration deadline. If you’ve moved, changed your name, or never registered, this is your last day. Delaware has no same day registration.

Oct 22

Early voting opens. October 22 to 24 and October 26 to 27, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Then October 28 through November 1, 11:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. No early voting Sunday the 25th. Use any early voting site in the county.

Nov 3

Election Day. 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. at your assigned polling place. Absentee ballots must be received by 8:00 p.m.

Check your registration and polling place

The Delaware Department of Elections runs the official lookup for your registration status, your polling place, your sample ballot, and absentee requests. Registering, checking your status, and requesting an absentee ballot all happen at the state’s voter portal.

Register or check your status Delaware Department of Elections

Confirm dates, hours, and early voting locations there before you head out. Absentee rules in Delaware have their own requirements and deadlines, so check the state’s absentee page rather than relying on us. Official information always wins over a campaign website, including this one.

Am I in District 2?

District 2 runs from the Pennsylvania line down to the Wilmington city line, and from the Red Clay Creek east past Concord Pike. It includes Talleyville, Fairfax, McDaniel Heights, Blue Rock Manor, Oak Lane Manor, Talleys Corner, the Branmar area, Centreville, Greenville, Montchanin, Rockland, Winterthur, Westover Hills, and neighborhoods in between.

If you’re in 19810, 19803, or 19807 you’re very likely in District 2, but district lines don’t follow ZIP codes exactly. Your sample ballot at the link above will confirm it.

What’s actually on this ballot

County council sets the county property tax rate, the sewer rates, and the county budget. That’s the seat. If you want to know how those numbers got where they are, we laid out the whole story of your tax bill, with every claim sourced. If you want to know what I’d do about it, that’s the plan.
