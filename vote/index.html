Dates to keep 2026

Oct 10

Voter registration deadline. If you have moved, changed your name, or never registered, this is your last day.

Early voting opens. Runs October 22 to 24, October 26 to 27, and October 28 through November 1, at any early voting site in your county.

Election Day. 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. at your assigned polling place. Absentee ballots must be received by 8:00 p.m.

Am I in District 2?

District 2 runs from the Pennsylvania line down to the Wilmington city line, and from the Red Clay Creek east past Concord Pike. It includes Talleyville, Fairfax, McDaniel Heights, Blue Rock Manor, Oak Lane Manor, Talleys Corner, the Branmar area, Centreville, Greenville, Montchanin, Rockland, Winterthur, Westover Hills, and neighborhoods in between.

If you are in 19810, 19803, or 19807 you are very likely in District 2, but the district lines do not follow ZIP codes exactly. Your sample ballot at the link above will confirm it.
